Why I built
Mizan.

A community zakat calculator for Lunch Money users.

My balances were already in Lunch Money.

I wanted to use them for my zakat calculation, so I built Mizan. You can check what belongs and edit a balance.

Add anything that isn't recorded, such as cash at home. It can't check your coat pockets, so that bit is still on you.

Check how the total adds up.

The breakdown lists each account and deduction, with edits labelled. The graphs use those same amounts. You can also compare methods or look through the monthly balances Lunch Money has available.

Choose the method you follow.

Check any balance marked "not current" before using the estimate. Confirm your zakat method with your scholar.

See the API documentation or browse other Lunch Money community tools.
