Why zakat

An obligation, not a nice-to-have.

Zakat is one of the five pillars of Islam. For Muslims whose wealth reaches the nisab and is held for a lunar year, giving 2.5% of it is a duty, owed to the people Allah names in the Quran.

The basics

Three ideas behind every calculation.

2.5%

The rate

Of your whole eligible wealth after debts, not just the part above the threshold.

Nisab

The threshold

The value of 85 g of gold or 595 g of silver. Below it, no zakat is due. Scholars differ on which metal to use.

Hawl

The year

Your wealth has to be held for one lunar year (354 days) before zakat on it is due.

Connect your budget.

An access token lets Mizan read your balances. It takes about a minute to make one.

Where are your balances?
  1. 1
    Make a token in Lunch Money

    Go to Settings → Developers and request a new access token. Name it “Mizan” so it’s easy to revoke later. Step-by-step guide

    Make a token in YNAB

    In YNAB, open Account Settings → Developer Settings and choose New Token. Enter your password, then copy the token while it’s shown. YNAB’s instructions

  2. 2
    Paste it here

  3. 3
    Revoke it when you’re done

    Lunch Money tokens can change data. Mizan never does, but deleting the token afterwards keeps things tidy.

    YNAB tokens can change data and don’t expire. Mizan never changes anything, but revoke the token in Developer Settings afterwards.

What happens to your token

  • It goes only to the Lunch Money APIthe YNAB API.
  • Mizan only makes read requests.
  • It’s cleared from the page once your balances arrive, and never saved.
