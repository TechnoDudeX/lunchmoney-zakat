Calculate your zakat from your Lunch Money balances

Mizan reads your account balances from Lunch Money, gets today's gold and silver prices, and estimates your zakat. Its balance history helps you review your hawl.

Connect your Lunch Money

  1. Go to my.lunchmoney.app/developers, and make an access token. Give it a name, for example "Zakat calculator".
  2. Paste the token here, and click Connect.

A community project. Lunch Money did not make it and does not support it.

Your data stays with you

  • Read only. This page only reads your data. It has no code that changes or deletes anything.
  • The page does not keep your token. Your token goes only to api.lunchmoney.dev, and the page forgets it after it reads your balances.
  • No server. This page has no server, so nothing is saved on a server.
  • Lunch Money tokens can change data. You can delete your token on the developers page when you finish.

Notes

  • When your total after debts is at or above the nisab, zakat is 2.5% of the whole total, once each lunar year.
  • Your home and your cars are not subject to zakat, so the calculator does not include them.
  • Scholars do not agree about the mortgage. Some deduct only the payment that is due now. Others deduct the payments that are due in the next 12 months.
  • Scholars do not agree about retirement accounts, and about gold jewellery that a woman wears. Ask about them before you include them.
  • If money in an account belongs to another person (for example your spouse), it is part of their zakat. Clear the box for that account.
  • Your choices (nisab, mortgage method, purity) are saved in this browser. Amounts and your token are never saved.
  • This is an estimate. Ask your imam or a zakat scholar to confirm the method that you follow.
