The calculation, in four steps

Do these steps on your zakat date.

  1. Add the value of all your zakatable wealth: cash, savings, gold, silver, investments, trade goods and money that people owe you.
  2. Subtract the debts that you can deduct.
  3. Compare the result with the nisab.
  4. Check your hawl, then pay 2.5% of the whole result if it is at or above the nisab and the relevant lunar year has passed. If it is below the nisab, no zakat is due on this calculation.

Understand the basics

What zakat is

Zakat is one of the 5 pillars of Islam (Bukhari 8). The Quran orders it together with prayer (2:43), and says that it purifies the giver (9:103).

For the kinds of wealth covered here, zakat depends on Muslim ownership, the nisab and a lunar year. Ownership and access also matter, especially for retirement accounts.

Where scholars differ

  • Age and mental capacity: these are conditions in the Hanafi school for zakat on wealth of the kind covered here. Other schools require a guardian to pay from the qualifying wealth of a child or a person who cannot manage it. NZF Canada follows the guardian-payment view.

The rate: 2.5%

The rate on cash, gold, silver and trade goods is 2.5%, which is 1/40 of the wealth. The hadith give the examples of 5 dirhams on 200 dirhams, and half a dinar on 20 dinars (Abu Dawud 1573, Bukhari 1454).

The rate is for 1 lunar year. For a solar year, NZF UK reports an AAOIFI rate of 2.577%, while recommending the lunar calendar. Mizan always uses 2.5%; it does not adjust the rate for a solar year.

The nisab

The nisab is the minimum wealth that makes zakat necessary. The hadith set it at 20 dinars of gold and 200 dirhams (5 awaq) of silver (Abu Dawud 1573, Bukhari 1447). Scholars change these old units to grams of pure metal:

GoldSilverExample source
85 g595 gNZF Canada
87.48 g612.36 gIslamic Relief Canada

To get the nisab in your currency, multiply the grams by today's price for 1 gram of pure metal. Mizan uses 85 g and 595 g, and you can change both weights.

Gold nisab or silver nisab

Authorities differ on which metal's value to use for cash. The prices of gold and silver change independently, so the choice can change whether your wealth reaches the threshold.

Mizan starts with the silver nisab. You can choose gold in Your estimate in the calculator, and "Compare all six methods" shows both.

The hawl: 1 lunar year

Zakat on cash, gold, silver and trade goods is due after 1 lunar year (hawl) of ownership (Abu Dawud 1573). A lunar year is 354 or 355 days. Your hawl starts on the date that your wealth first gets to the nisab, and that date becomes your zakat date each year. Profit and growth from your wealth join the hawl of that wealth.

What does not count

Zakat does not apply to things that you keep for your own use (Bukhari 1463):

  • the home that you live in
  • your car, clothes, furniture and household items
  • the tools and equipment of your work
  • the value of a rental property (count the rent money that you saved and still have)

A property that you bought to sell is trade goods, so count its value.

Jewellery

Gold and silver held for investment or trade are zakatable. Personal-use jewellery has different rules. Gemstones kept for personal use are generally exempt; gemstones held as trading stock are business assets. See Zakat Foundation of America's jewellery guidance.

ViewHeld by
All gold and silver jewellery is zakatable, also jewellery that a woman wears.The Hanafi school
Jewellery intended for customary, permitted personal use is generally exempt. Conditions differ, including treatment of excessive amounts and jewellery held as savings. Occasional use can qualify; not wearing an item every day does not alone make it zakatable.The Maliki, Shafi'i and Hanbali schools; see Islamic Relief UK's comparison.

How to value gold and silver

Count only the weight of the metal, and change it to pure metal. For gold, multiply the weight by the karat and divide by 24. For example, 18 karat gold is 75% gold. For silver, multiply the weight by the fineness. For example, sterling silver is 0.925. Mizan does this under Add other assets.

Retirement accounts

Scholars do not agree about retirement accounts such as an RRSP, a 401(k), an IRA or a pension. The main question is whether you own and control the money before you take it out.

ViewHeld by
Pay annually on the portion you could access, after withdrawal taxes, penalties and fees.One view described by Zakat Foundation of America.
Restricted funds are not subject to annual zakat while restrictions prevent full control. Ask how zakat applies when funds become accessible.Another view in the same source. It distinguishes unrestricted accounts from accounts with withdrawal restrictions.
For a defined-contribution pension, count the fund's zakatable assets. Defined-benefit pensions are treated differently.NZF UK's calculation guide, pensions section.

Mizan counts a retirement account at its full balance and adds a note. If you follow the first view, type the amount after tax and penalties in the account list. If you follow the second view, clear the box.

Your circumstances

Debts and the mortgage

Debt deductions are a matter of scholarly disagreement, including whether debts reduce zakatable wealth at all. Follow the rules of your chosen authority. These are examples of different approaches:

ViewHeld byIn Mizan
Deduct only the payment that is due now, for example this month's mortgage payment.AMJA (fatwa 21598) and others"Deduct the next payment"
Deduct up to the next lunar year's capital repayments on a long-term loan, excluding interest. NZF UK advises this deduction when paying zakat would affect the ability to repay.NZF UK, with conditions"Deduct the next 12 months", using only the deductible amount per payment
Do not deduct the mortgage debt.Zakat Foundation of America"Do not deduct"

Mizan selects credit card balances by default. Review and edit each balance to include only eligible debt under your method. To follow a method that allows no debt deductions, clear every debt checkbox, leave other debts empty and choose "Do not deduct" for the mortgage. Avoid deducting the same debt twice.

Family members

Each person pays zakat on the wealth that they own. Calculate the nisab and the zakat for each person separately. A husband does not have to pay zakat on his wife's wealth or jewellery. Another person, for example a husband, can pay your zakat for you with your permission.

In Mizan, clear the box for any account that belongs to another person.

Who receives zakat, and when

Zakat goes only to the 8 groups in Quran 9:60: the poor, the needy, the people who collect and manage zakat, people whose hearts are to be brought near, to free slaves and captives, people in debt, the cause of Allah, and the stranded traveller.

  • Pay on your zakat date, without unnecessary delay. Zakat that you did not pay stays a debt.
  • Many people pay in Ramadan, because the Prophet was most generous in Ramadan (Bukhari 6). Ramadan is not a necessary time.
  • You can pay before your zakat date (Abu Dawud 1624). The schools give different limits for how early. On your zakat date, calculate the correct amount and pay the difference.
